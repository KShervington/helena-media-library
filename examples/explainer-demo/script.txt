# hook
This repo got forty thousand stars in six days — and nobody could explain why.

# proof
It's a vision model small enough to run on your laptop.

# mechanism
A vision model can't look at a picture. So it chops the image into two hundred and fifty-six tiny tiles, like cutting a photo into puzzle pieces.

# tokens
Each tile becomes a number — a kind of word. So the picture turns into a sentence the language model can read.
